NL → Regex → Automata

We present privacy preserving AI powered regular expression to finite automata visualizer that converts natural language descriptions into accurate regular expressions and instantly displays both NFA and DFA all without a single byte of data leaving the user’s device. By integrating Google’s Gemma-2B large language model through Ollama running entirely on the local machine, our system eliminates dependency on cloud APIs, ensuring complete data privacy and zero internet requirement after initial setup. Unlike existing solutions that either rely on paid cloud LLMs (compromising privacy) or lack visual automata output, our tool combines state-of-the-art local artificial intelligence with a beautifully redesigned, centered, white themed interface making the complex concepts of regular expressions and finite automata intuitive and accessible to students for the very first time. This work represents a significant leap forward in educational tools for the Theory of Automata
